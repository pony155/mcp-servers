# Aseprite Top Down Character Production

Define the camera angle, direction ordering, ground contact point, overlap rules, and mirrored-direction policy first. Preserve the same footprint and perceived height across directions while allowing deliberate silhouette changes for depth.

Keep equipment on separable layers when it must vary independently. Compare matching motion phases across directions, verify handedness and lighting before mirroring, and validate tag names, pivots, collision masks, events, and export ordering as one set.
