# Aseprite Environment Prop Production

Confirm viewing angle, footprint, scale reference, lighting direction, states, interaction point, and collision intent. Keep static structure aligned across intact, active, damaged, and destroyed variants unless displacement is deliberate.

Use explicit tags for animated states and slices for pivots or interaction regions. Generate collision masks only from the agreed layer or final alpha, review them against the sprite, then validate and export all variants with consistent naming and dimensions.
