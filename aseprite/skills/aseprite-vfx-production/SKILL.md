# Aseprite Vfx Production

Establish the effect's gameplay event, duration, origin, maximum footprint, background conditions, and additive or ordinary blending expectations. Design clear anticipation, peak, breakup, and dissipation phases; do not distribute visual energy evenly across all frames.

Keep the palette and particle count controlled. Review the animation over light and dark backgrounds, inspect first/last continuity for loops, and validate empty frames and bounds. Export pivots or slices at the gameplay origin and document whether frames may be trimmed.
