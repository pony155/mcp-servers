# Aseprite Accessibility Review

Inspect native-scale and intended-scale previews on representative backgrounds. Check whether states remain distinguishable without hue alone, whether important silhouettes survive low contrast, and whether animation timing creates rapid high-contrast flashing or unreadable motion.

Use exact palette and frame comparisons to support findings, but avoid claiming medical or standards compliance from a sprite-only review. Rank issues by gameplay impact, propose the smallest palette, shape, icon, or timing correction, and preserve the original art direction where possible.
