# Aseprite Portrait Expression Production

Approve one neutral identity anchor before creating variants. Keep skull shape, eye spacing, nose placement, hair mass, camera angle, crop, palette roles, and lighting fixed unless the brief explicitly changes them. Express emotion through brows, eyelids, pupils, mouth corners, jaw, and small pose offsets in that order.

Organize expressions with stable names and equal canvas placement. Reuse linked cels for invariant regions only when later edits should propagate; unlink before expression-specific changes. Compare variants against neutral, review at native scale and in the dialogue UI, then validate palette, alpha, dimensions, and export naming. Blinks and lip shapes should be tagged separately from emotional states.
